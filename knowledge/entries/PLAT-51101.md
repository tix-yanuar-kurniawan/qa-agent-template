## Summary

This QA story covers Phase 1 development of the Unified Messaging Platform (UMP), a centralized multi-tenant messaging infrastructure for group entities such as Blibli, tiket, Ranch, and GKS. UMP addresses fragmented messaging systems by providing shared WhatsApp and SMS delivery with a dashboard for SMS fallback and template configuration. Phase 1 focuses on building core UMP services with multi-tenancy so entities can send messages through integrated third-party providers.

## Key Points

- UMP is a centralized, multi-tenant messaging platform initially supporting WhatsApp and SMS, with future extensibility toward Email and Push Notifications.
- Goals include centralizing message delivery, achieving cost efficiency through consolidated vendor contracts, and enabling centralized message orchestration across entities.
- Phase 1 scope: UMP Service for message sending requests to third parties, UMP Dashboard for SMS fallback from WhatsApp templates and SMS template creation, and integration with multiple third-party WhatsApp/SMS providers.
- Phase 2 will integrate Blibli and Tiket to send WhatsApp and SMS through UMP after core services are complete.
- Phase 3 (TBC) may add Email and Push Notifications channels and integrate Ranch and Dekoruma, plus centralized message orchestration for cross-entity cross selling.
- Epic scope is development of Phase 1 only.

# Context

In the current ecosystem, multiple business entities such as Blibli, tiket, Ranch and GKS (Group-level services currently supporting UNM and ULP) require reliable, scalable, and cost-effective communication channels to reach their customers. However, messaging systems are fragmented across teams, with inconsistent rules, limited visibility, and duplicated integration efforts.

The Unified Messaging Platform (UMP) is being developed as a centralized, multi-tenant messaging infrastructure to serve as a shared backbone for all group entities. UMP will initially support WhatsApp and SMS, with extensibility toward Email and Push Notifications. Each entity will be able to access messaging services under its own business use cases.

# Goals

1. **Centralize message delivery service** across all group companies to reduce duplication of effort, increase reliability, and improve visibility.
2. **Achieve cost efficiency** by consolidating vendor contracts, optimizing routing logic, and eliminating redundant platform development across entities.
3. **Enable centralized message orchestration** across entities to power cross-sell, upsell, lifecycle marketing, and group-level CRM initiatives — unlocking ecosystem value through coordinated, data-driven outreach.

# High-Level Project Scope

1. Build UMP Service that takes message sending request from entity, to integrated 3rd party.
2. UMP Dashboard to configure SMS fallback messages from WhatsApp templates, and create SMS templates.
3. Integrate with multiple 3rd party services to send WhatsApp messages or SMS.

### Project Phases

The project will be split into several phases:

1. **Phase 1 - Building of Unified Messaging Platform**

    1. Building the core services of UMP. Enabling multi-tenancy system where entities can leverage message sending capabilities through this service, instead of their own.
    2. This phase will support 2 kinds of message sending service:

        1. Whatsapp, and
        2. SMS

2. **Phase 2 - Entity Integration with UMP**

    1. After the core service is completed, entities can integrate with UMP to enable message sending through UMP instead of their services.
    2. In this phase, entities are expected to send Whatsapp and SMS messages through UMP.
    3. This phase will prioritise the following 2 entities to be integrated:

        1. Blibli, and
        2. Tiket.

3. **Phase 3 - Future Improvements (TBC)**

    1. In the future improvement phases, UMP will consider centralising more message channels, including:

        1. Email, and
        2. Push Notifications.

    2. Aside from that, more entities will be planned for integration, including:

        1. Ranch, and
        2. Dekoruma.

    3. Enabling centralised message orchestration to further leverage cross-entity cross selling.
