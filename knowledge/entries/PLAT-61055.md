# PLAT-61055 — Shake to Report Setting
> **Related:** [[NCT-22112]] · [[NCT-22110]] · [[PLAT-61053]] · [[PLAT-61042]] · [[PLAT-61601]]

## PRD Summary
Adds a "Report an Issue" entry to the Account page of the Android and iOS apps
so both logged-in and guest users can reach the existing Shake to Share
(Shake to Report) flow without shaking. The implementation lives in the core
stories NCT-22112 (Android) and NCT-22110 (iOS), which have identical text:

- "Report An Issue" row in Settings, directly below Location, for login and
  non-login users. Tapping it opens a bottom sheet. Choosing the report option
  opens the existing Shake to Share flow **without** a screenshot. A shake
  keeps the existing screenshot behaviour.
- Shake to Share is enabled by default, the email recipient field is empty by
  default, and the floating window hides itself after 4 seconds.
- Tapping the app version name no longer opens the Shake to Share pop-up.

## Figma Design Summary
- **Account › Settings** (5122-8766): the rows are Language, Currency,
  Notification, Location (toggle), then **Report an Issue** (with a chevron),
  then the Blibli Tiket Account Center card.
- **Bottom sheet** (5119-6208): the title is "Found an issue with the app?", followed by a blue info banner reading "You shook your
  phone and activated the Shake to Report feature. Tell us what went wrong.".
  Below it is a "Report an issue" row with the subtitle "If you found any problem on the app, share your
  feedback so we can fix it." and a chevron. The last row is "Shake to Report" with a toggle (ON).
- **Existing setting dialog** (4857-117610, the asset reference on the epic):
  "Report an Issue" sheet with a close X, the copy "…**shake your phone** to
  easily report it…", and a Shake to Report toggle (OFF).

## Key Learnings
- **The epic body is not the live scope.** The epic and its PLAT stories
  (PLAT-61053 Android, PLAT-61042 iOS) say the row opens "the existing shake to
  report toggle setting dialog" with no development. Both PLAT stories are
  **Invalid** (comment 2026-10-08: "changes coming from core"). The NCT core
  stories were added under the epic and specify more: a new bottom sheet, a
  flow without a screenshot, ON by default, an empty recipient, the 4 s auto-hide,
  and removal of the version-name trigger. The NCT stories are the live scope.
- **The bottom-sheet design carries the shake-triggered banner.** Node 5119-6208
  is the sheet linked for the Account tap, yet its banner says "You shook your
  phone…". Whether the banner is hidden on the menu entry is `UNKNOWN`; ask the PM.
- **Undefined terms:** "floating window" and where the tappable "version name" lives. Both are `UNKNOWN`.
- **Default ON for existing users is unspecified.** A user who disabled Shake
  to Share before the update may be kept OFF or reset to ON. This is `UNKNOWN`.
- The epic is labelled "General", but both live stories are Android and iOS only. There is no web or Panel scope.
