# PLAT-62520 — [iOS] Prevent ULP Routing When WebView Redirects in the Background

## PRD Summary

The ULP (Blibli Tiket Rewards) WebView sits behind the Rewards bottom tab and is created
at launch, even if the user never opens it. When iOS terminates its process (memory
pressure, time in background, or a WebKit crash), the WebView recovers and reloads
`tiket.com/rewards`. That URL redirects to `bliblitiketrewards.com`, and the redirect fired
the app's ULP routing, so the user was thrown onto ULP from wherever they were.

**THEN:** the app must not trigger ULP routing while the ULP page is not visible, and the
user stays on their current page. When ULP **is** visible, existing routing is unchanged.

PRs: tiketiphone #19500, lego-ios #219 (comment by Ihsan Husnul Aqidah). Status Ready
for QA on 2026-11-11.

## Key Learnings

- A never-opened ULP does not reload after a crash
  until Rewards is tapped. After a visit, it reloads in the background straight away.
- The natural trigger (iOS idle-exit about 4 min into background) is real but
  unreliable.
