# PLAT-54053 — [BE] Adjustment on claim end date
> **Type:** Story · **Status:** Done · **Priority:** Medium · **Parent:** [[PLAT-54052]]
> **Related:** [[PLAT-54052]]
> **Jira:** https://borobudur.atlassian.net/browse/PLAT-54053

## Summary
This backend story changes claim end date calculation from a fixed absolute date to a relative period of N days after the user's effective mission end date. When a user completes a mission, the claim end date is calculated as the effective mission end date plus the claim period (N days), with users able to claim until 23:59 of the last claim duration day. The effective mission end date is either the global end date (no dynamic end period) or MIN(user_enrollment_date + Dynamic End Period, global_end_date).

## Key Points
- Claim configuration changes from fixed absolute claim end date to relative `claim_duration_days` (e.g., 7 days); user can claim up to 23:59 of the last claim duration day.
- Without Dynamic End Period: effective end date = Global end date.
- With Dynamic End Period: effective end date = MIN(user_enrollment_date + Dynamic End Period, global_end_date).
- Claim end date = Effective mission end date + claim_duration_days.
- Example (no dynamic end): Global end 20 Aug, claim duration 7 days → claim until 27 Aug 23:59.
- Example (dynamic before global): User enrolls 1 Aug, Dynamic End Period 30 → ends 31 Aug, global end 31 Dec, claim 7 days → effective end 31 Aug, claim until 7 Sep 23:59.
- Example (global overrides dynamic): User enrolls 10 Dec, Dynamic End Period 30 → 9 Jan, global end 31 Dec, claim 7 days → effective end 31 Dec, claim until 7 Jan 23:59.

## Details
**Given**

A mission has:

* Global end date
* (Optional) Dynamic End Period (e.g., 30 days after enrollment)
* Claim period defined as **N days**

**When**

A user completes the mission

**Then**

The claim end date should be calculated as:

* Based on the **user’s effective mission end date**
* Plus **N days claim period**

**Additional Details**

### **1. Change in Claim Configuration**

**Current:**

* Claim end date = fixed date (absolute)

**New:**

* Claim end = **relative (N days)**

    * Example: `claim_duration_days = 7`

* User can claim up to 23:59 of the last claim duration days

### **2. Effective Mission End Date Logic**

The system should determine the **effective end date per user**:

#### **Case A: No Dynamic End Period**

* Effective end date = **Global end date**

#### **Case B: With Dynamic End Period**

* Effective end date =
  **MIN (user_enrollment_date + Dynamic End Period, global_end_date)**

### **3. Claim End Date Calculation**

Claim end date = **Effective mission end date + claim_duration_days**

## **Examples**

#### **Scenario 1: No Dynamic End Period**

* Global end: 20 Aug
* Claim duration: 7 days

→ Claim until: **27 Aug 23:59**

---

#### **Scenario 2: Dynamic End Period Applies (Before Global End)**

* User enrolls: 1 Aug
* Dynamic End Period 30 → ends 31 Aug
* Global end: 31 Dec
* Claim duration: 7 days

→ Effective end: 31 Aug
→ Claim until: **7 Sep 23:59**

---

#### **Scenario 3: Global End Overrides Dynamic**

* User enrolls: 10 Dec
* Dynamic End Period: 30 → 9 Jan
* Global end: 31 Dec
* Claim duration: 7 days

→ Effective end: 31 Dec
→ Claim until: **7 Jan 23:59**
