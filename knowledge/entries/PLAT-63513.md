# PLAT-63513 — [BE] Handle PAH, Refund, Reschedule for Zero Flexible Payment
> **Related:** [[PLAT-59743]] · [[PLAT-54713]] · [[PLAT-53384]] · [[PLAT-57759]]

## PRD Summary

Standalone `[BE]` story (no parent, no links, status Ready for QA), QA verification only: "There are no BE changes". Zero Flexible Payment orders reach Issued without being Paid, so earning is driven by the order lifecycle. Pending at Issued, issued at Finished, cancelled if Cancelled/Refunded before issue, existing reversal if already issued. That part restates [[PLAT-59743]]. The story's own content is a BAU table for ZFP orders:

- **Refund:** all earning points and cashback cancelled; IPC revoked, or deducted from the refund amount if already used.
- **Reschedule:** earning points and cashback follow the new date.
- **PAH:** no earning points at all.
- **meta_id and affiliate restriction:** still apply as today.
- **Rebooking:** old order's points cancelled; new points published under the new order ID.

## Key Learnings

- **Lifecycle bullets are [[PLAT-59743]]'s scope, not repeated here.** Issued→Finished, cancel-pending and refund-pending belong to PLAT-59743. This story covers the BAU table.
- **Not applicable: "points already issued, then cancel/refund → existing reversal".** tiket's `finished | refund | reschedule` are mutually exclusive, so a Finished ZFP order cannot be refunded.
- **IPC on ZFP is the sharpest gap.** IPC is credited instantly on a paid order. The story does not say when IPC is credited on a ZFP order, which has no Paid event. The "deduct used IPC from the refund amount" rule also meets a ZFP refund amount that can be IDR 0. The shortfall question is open: ask the PIC what recovers the rest.
- **The affiliate restriction ([[PLAT-53384]]) is triggered by "payment successfully completed".** ZFP orders are not Paid at Issued, so the restriction may silently not fire on the lifecycle-driven path.
- **Undefined terms:** `meta_id` (not in the story or the vault). PAH + ZFP on one order is not explained either. "Follow the new date" for reschedule does not say whether pending moves to a new order ID.
- **Two-step refund:** the story names only Refunded. Nothing issues while refund is requested; the request-time state is `UNKNOWN`.
- **Related prior knowledge:** [[PLAT-54713]] refund/reschedule/rebooking/IPC semantics and state machine; [[PLAT-53384]] tag_aff_id trigger.
