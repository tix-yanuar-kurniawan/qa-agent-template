# PLAT-61138 — Family Group in Passenger Data
> **Related:** [[PLAT-61765]] · [[PLAT-61780]] · [[PLAT-61782]] · [[PLAT-61784]] · [[PLAT-61785]] · [[PLAT-61787]] · [[PLAT-61788]] · [[PLAT-61789]] · [[PLAT-61791]] · [[PLAT-61792]] · [[PLAT-61793]] · [[PLAT-61796]] · [[PLAT-60403]] · [[PLAT-59157]] · [[PLAT-57210]]

## PRD Summary

Lets a user group saved passenger profiles into **family groups**. The goal is to
capture family and group-size signals before search. Source: the Confluence PRD
"[PRD] Aspirational Family - Family Group in Passenger Data" (page 4956061735,
v34, status DRAFT). The epic body only links it. The 23 child stories have no
descriptions, only titles and subtasks.

PRD requirements:

1. **Scope and limits**
   - A family has 2–10 profiles; the maximum of 10 is configurable on the BE.
   - An account can have up to 5 families; the 5 is configurable.
   - A profile can belong to several families.
   - A member needs a name, gender, date of birth and nationality.
   - The category comes from date of birth and today's date: Infant 0–23 months,
     Child 2–11 years, Adult 12–65 years, Senior >65 years.
   - Only one family can be the default (main) family. The first family created
     becomes the default automatically, and the default can be moved to another
     family. Deleting the default family leaves the account with no default.
2. **My Account menu.** A new "Family Group" menu on mWeb and apps only, with a
   new label. Clicking it sends a tracker (`click` / `myAccount` /
   `family Group`) and opens the Family Group page.
3. **Passenger list preview.** Shows the default family and a manage CTA. The
   create entry is hidden at 5 families.
4. **Landing page.** With no family it shows a static page with an animation and
   a create CTA. Otherwise it lists each family's name, illustration, member
   count and main flag. The create CTA is disabled at 5 families.
5. **Create.**
   - Family name: letters and spaces only, at most 30 characters, mandatory,
     unique within the account.
   - The main toggle is ON for the first family or when there is no default,
     and OFF otherwise.
   - Suggestions list the main profile first, then up to 3 others. They are
     ranked by frequency (more than once in the past year), then recency, then
     last update. If the data system fails, they fall back to last-updated order.
   - The main profile is preselected unless its data is incomplete, and it can
     be removed.
   - An incomplete secondary profile must be completed before it can be picked.
   - The family needs at least one adult and 2–10 members.
   - "Add others" lists up to 10 more profiles, with a search.
   - "Add new member" has first name (with an existing-profile picker), last
     name, date of birth, sex and nationality. Saving runs a duplicate-name check.
   - The illustration places children and infants in the front row. Its frame is
     small, medium or large by member count.
6. **Edit.**
   - The family name and members can be changed.
   - Turning the toggle ON moves the default here. On the default family the
     toggle is disabled.
   - Removing the second-last member shows a warning that offers to delete the
     family (confirm or decline).
   - A family with no adult is not allowed.
7. **Edit passenger.**
   - Lists the families the profile belongs to, each with a view CTA.
   - Has a create CTA, hidden at 5 families.
   - The profile can be added to any family that is not full ("full" info is
     shown), including several families.
   - An incomplete profile cannot be added to a family.
   - Deleting the profile warns about its family membership, removes it from
     those families, and deletes any family left with fewer than 2 members.
8. **Distribute family composition** to verticals: whether the user has a family,
   and the default family's total, per-category counts, and profile ids with dates
   of birth.
9. **My Account nudge.**
   - Dynamic nudge: shown when the user has no family and at least 2 secondary
     profiles were booked together in at least 2 orders in the past year. It
     shows the number of profiles and orders. Its CTA opens a create form with
     the recommended profiles ticked.
   - Static nudge: segment copy comes from discovery and the survey, with general
     copy as the fallback. Its CTA opens the landing page.
   - X closes the nudge until the next visit. After 3 dismissals in total it is
     never shown again.
10. **Audit log.** `create_family`, `update_family` and `delete_family`, with
    timestamp, sourceIp, sourceIdentity, targetIdentity, the family and profile
    ids, and Success or Fail.

## Figma Design Summary

Only the "Family Member - Account Page" frame was read
(13,605×23,865). Its two file comments are minor: alternative button names, and
"add notes responsive screen".

- **Saved Passenger Data**
  - The family section reads "Travel together, book faster".
  - With a family: "You have N family." and a **Manage** button that opens the
    Family Member page.
  - With no family: "Group passengers as a family and pick everyone in one tap at
    checkout." and a **Create** button.
- **Family Member page**
  - With no family: the title "Family Member", "Create a family group and easily
    book a trip for your family.", the benefits **Personalized** and **Special
    Promo**, and **Add New Family**.
  - With one or more families: "You have 1 Family Group" and a card showing the
    name and "4 members".
- **Add a new family form**
  - Fields: Family Detail (Family Name) and Family Member (an illustration frame,
    then one row per profile with a pencil, name, "(You)" on the main profile,
    the category and a checkbox), then "Add others".
  - Footer: "Add minimum 2 person", the "Set as main family" toggle, and "N
    member" next to **Create Family** (disabled below 2). The summary reads "You
    add 2 adult, 2 child, 1 infant, 1 elder".
  - Success lands on the Family Member page "anchoring to the new family".
    Errors use the CEH.
- **Add member sheet**
  - Shows "You've added 3/10", a "Search by member name" box, and "Only 10
    person are shown. Search by name to find others.", plus **Add new member**
    and **Add**.
  - Design note: members added from the sheet appear below the selected ones and
    replace the unselected ones. The maximum is 10.
- **Duplicate dialog.** "We notice you already have this passenger data. Name: …
  Date of birth: … Do you want to add this Jane Doe instead?" with **No, it's
  different** and **Yes, it's the same**. A sticky note says the check runs when
  the first name or surname changes.
- **Incomplete profiles**
  - Secondary profile: a "Complete the member data" sheet with Date of Birth, Sex
    and Nationality required, plus the inline errors "Enter the date of birth
    listed on the ID or passport." and "Choose the sex as shown on the ID or
    passport."
  - Main profile: an "Edit main profile" sheet. Name and DOB are read-only ("You
    can complete them in Account Center"), and Sex and Nationality can be edited.
- **Delete.** The dialog reads "Delete Raharjos family? When you book for this
  family, you must manually enter the passenger data one by one." with **No,
  cancel** and **Yes, delete**. The snackbar reads "Successfully delete Raharjos
  Family."
- **States:** Load Error ("Unable to load member suggestion"), Unselect member,
  and a dynamic illustration frame for 0–5, 6–7 and 8–10 people in a row.
- **My Account nudge** (Xeno, `[NO HISTORY ORDER] GENERAL`)
  - Copy: "Pick who's in your family/ group of friends. Get personalize and
    relevant recommendation!" with a **Set up** button that opens the Family
    Member page.
  - The logic sticky repeats the PRD's survey segments (Friend/Solo, Family
    couple, Family kids) and the rule of at most 3 dismissals.

## Key Learnings

- **Reconciled the source.**
  - There is no change log or Q&A table. The 25 inline comments are the decision
    record, and all but 3 typo fixes are still **open**.
  - Open questions:
    - Can the first family's toggle be switched OFF?
    - Category boundaries such as 23 months + 1 day and 11 years + 1 month, and
      whether Senior should be "elder".
    - "Doesn't edit-passenger 7.3 contradict 1.3?" — create lets the user
      complete an incomplete profile, while edit-passenger blocks it.
    - Distribution when families exist but none is the default.
  - The Jira comment's task list strikes out "FE side bar". With "mobile and
    apps only", that means no menu on desktop.
- **Story-level scope drift.**
  - `[FE] Passenger list - Preview Family Group` (PLAT-61781) is **Invalid**,
    while the iOS and Android twins are live. So the passenger-list family
    section is apps-only.
  - The landing page still names Passenger List as a create entry point for
    every platform. Whether Responsive shows any family entry on
    `/myaccount/passengers` is an open question.
  - Landing, create and edit have **only `[FE]` stories**, with no iOS or Android
    twin. The assumption is that the
    apps open the web page in a webview; this is unconfirmed.
- **Requirement 8 (distribute family composition) has no child story.**
  - It has no endpoint.
  - The PRD also does not say how verticals consume the data: an API, an event,
    or `profiles/b2c` (see [[PLAT-60351]]).
- **Design vs PRD vocabulary.**
  - The design labels the oldest category **Elder**, while the PRD says
    **Senior**. Check which word the build uses.
  - The design's main-profile sheet sends name and DOB edits to Account Center,
    which the PRD does not say.
- **Undefined behaviour.**
  - Does "adult" in the at-least-one-adult rule include **Senior**? Is a
    senior + child family allowed?
  - What happens when an edit to a profile's DOB leaves a family with no adult?
  - Which wins when the dynamic and static nudges both qualify?
  - Is the family-name uniqueness check case-insensitive?
  - Does deleting a family through the profile-delete cascade write a
    `delete_family` audit entry?
  - What does "Adding new label" on the menu mean, and what is its copy?
  - Is the profile preselected when the create CTA is opened from edit
    passenger?
  - Does the automatic removal of passengers unused for 18 months (an existing rule
    shown on the passenger list) cascade like a manual delete and drop families below
    2 members?
- **Passport renewal banner.** The design shows the passport renewal banner
  ([[PLAT-57210]]) on the same list, alongside the family section.
