# PLAT-52883 — [Panel] Top Spender 2.0 Gamification Dashboard Development
> **Type:** Story · **Status:** In QA · **Priority:** Medium · **Parent:** [[PLAT-54035]]
> **Related:** [[PLAT-54035-v2]] · [[PLAT-54035]]
> **Jira:** https://borobudur.atlassian.net/browse/PLAT-52883

## Summary

This story revamps the Top Spender Program into a high-engagement gamification platform with a real-time leaderboard, coin-based rewards, missions, and personalized competition mechanics to drive increased GBV and user retention. The initiative aims to make tiket.com the primary booking platform for informal travel agents, consolidating their spend and increasing platform stickiness. Backend acceptance criteria define core features for campaign configuration, eligibility, enrollment, rewards, moderation, and coin management.

## Key Points

- Campaign Configuration (Start & End Lifecycle Management).
- Dynamic Content & CMS Configuration.
- Coin Rate Calculation per Vertical.
- Product & User Eligibility Rules (Exclusion Handling).
- User Enrollment (Join Top Spender) & Welcome Bonus.
- Onboarding Success Notification.
- Campaign Finalization & Reward Claim Flow.
