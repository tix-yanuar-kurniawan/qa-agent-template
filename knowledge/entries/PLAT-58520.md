# PLAT-58520 — [FE] TIKETCUAN Affiliate Leaderboard
> **Related:** [[PLAT-58521]] · [[PLAT-58522]] · [[PLAT-58596]] · [[PLAT-59034]] · [[PLAT-53387]]

## PRD Summary

Part of the **tiket CUAN 15TIMEWA!** campaign. Registered affiliates get a
leaderboard and milestone-progression page showing their order progress,
unlockable medal tiers and estimated extra commission.

Three sections:

1. **Header & campaign banner** — campaign branding (*tiket CUAN 15TIMEWA*), the
   leaderboard section header (*Top Affiliates*), and a dynamic **last-updated
   timestamp** (`Last updated 31 Jul 2023 - 18:45:00` in the mock).
2. **User status banner — conditional on affiliate registration.**
   - *Participant* (existing affiliate): name, current tier, completed orders,
     earned-incentive badge, plus a progress nudge
     (`You need 20 orders to get the next incentive!`).
   - *Non-participant* (non-affiliate), shown **strictly** to non-affiliates:
     tagline `Earn millions from the comfort of your home.` /
     `Cuan sampai jutaan tanpa keluar rumah.`, and a `Join Affiliate` /
     `Gabung Affiliate` CTA into affiliate onboarding.
3. **Milestone tier leaderboards** — five tiers in order Crystal, Ruby, Emerald,
   Sapphire, Amethyst. Each has a badge icon, a tier title, an incentive banner
   (`Extra Incentive: IDR 1,400,000` / `Insentif Tambahan: IDR 1.400.000`) and a
   ranking table of affiliate names against completed orders
   (`Completed Orders` / `Pesanan Selesai`).

## Figma Design Summary

- **Two boards**: `Milestone Board Desktop` (11,255 × 5,999) lays each tier's
  ranking out in two side-by-side table columns; `Milestone Board Mobile`
  (4,857 × 4,079) stacks the same sections in one column. Frames are explicitly
  labelled *Milestone Board - Participant* and *Milestone Board - Non Participant*.
- **Tier empty state** (not in the PRD text): a tier with no ranked affiliates
  keeps its badge, title, incentive banner and column headers, and renders one
  row reading `Be the first to reach the top!` /
  `Jadilah yang pertama mencapai puncak!`.
- **Locale-specific number formatting**: EN `IDR 1,400,000` (commas) vs ID
  `IDR 1.400.000` (dots) — the same value, different separator.
- **Table column headers**: EN `Top Affiliates` / `Completed Orders`; ID
  `Top Affiliator` / `Pesanan Selesai`. The PRD says "Top Affiliate Name", which
  no screen uses.
- **Own-rank treatment**: a dedicated `Your rank` asset plus an *Own's rank hover
  State*, so the signed-in affiliate's row is visually distinguished.
- **Name abbreviation**: every mocked row shows first name plus initials
  (`Agung N. B.`, `Novita O.`, `Ernest D. M.`). No screen shows a full surname.
- **Entry points** (separate Figma page, own PIC and own status): homepage
  product icon, vertical insertion, floating button and a single banner
  (`Earn Up to 500rb per transaction` + *Get commission*).

## Key Learnings

- **The design is mid-flight, so exact copy is not final.** The Anatomy
  frame carries a status header: *Design status: In progress*, **DS Checking:
  Hold**, **Illustration: Hold**, **Copywriting: Hold**.
- **The Figma comment thread is the change log this epic doesn't have**, and it
  is where two things got settled and three did not.:
  - *Settled* — comment #60/#59: EN uses `Top Affiliates` ("Affiliate" singular,
    "Affiliates" plural); `Affiliator` is Indonesian-only, so ID keeps
    `Top Affiliator`. The asymmetric pair is intentional, not a typo.
  - *Open* — #66 and #67: *"Button ini mau bawa user ke
    mana?"* The **Join Affiliate CTA destination is unresolved**; it is only
    known to leave the page into onboarding.
  - *Open* — #64 (5 replies): the non-participant card copy is still being
    reworked toward "log in and register as an affiliate to join the campaign".
  - *Open* — #58 *"ganti 150k"*, #51 *"perlu add campaign date"*, #49 *"need KV"*:
    the incentive figures and campaign dates were still moving.
  - *Requested, not committed* — #65 *"add: pending orders"* and #57 (an extra
    milestone card). Not in any child story.
- **The epic is FE-only.** Children
  are `[FE] Solutioning` (Blocked), `[FE] Development`, `[FE] P0 TC Execution`
  and `[QA] Test` — there is **no `[BE]` child story**. Consequences: no stated
  source for the last-updated timestamp, and **no completed-order
  thresholds for the five tiers** even though the nudge copy implies they exist.
- **No entry point path exists anywhere in the epic or its children.** The Figma
  Entry Point page draws the four homepage entry points, but that page has its
  own PIC and its own design status, so its scope is uncertain.
- **The epic body is the stale half of the pair.** It describes only a
  "User Status Card (Logged-in User State)"; both `[FE] Solutioning` and
  `[FE] Development` describe the fuller two-state *User Status Banner*
  including the non-participant variant. The children are current scope.
- **The logged-out state is undefined.** The epic defines affiliate and
  non-affiliate, never a guest.
- **The page is read-only presentation:** nothing here loses money, tokens or
  data. The money risk sits in tier assignment, which cannot be specified until
  thresholds are defined.
