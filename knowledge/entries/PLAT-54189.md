# PLAT-54189 — [Panel] Adjust claim end date field from date to number of days
> **Type:** Story · **Status:** Done · **Priority:** Medium · **Parent:** [[PLAT-54052]]
> **Related:** [[PLAT-54052]]
> **Jira:** https://borobudur.atlassian.net/browse/PLAT-54189

## Summary

On the mission dashboard, the Claim end date and Claim end time fields are removed and replaced with a single Reward Claim Window (Days) field. This changes the claim window configuration from separate date and time inputs to a number-of-days value.

## Key Points

- Remove the Claim end date field from the mission dashboard.
- Remove the Claim end time field from the mission dashboard.
- Replace both fields with Reward Claim Window (Days).
- Figma reference: https://www.figma.com/design/csU8ZwfVQWxyAbu8Ea6ynQ/-SSoT--Loyalty-Dashboard?node-id=6254-35934&t=uddWi3LmV9hGBQYc-4

## Details
On the mission dashboard, remove these 2 fields:

1. Claim end date
2. Claim end time

And replace it with:

* Reward Claim Window (Days)
