# PLAT-54718 — Tiket Mission Improvement - Mission Pending Progress
> **Source:** Jira Epic · **Jira:** https://borobudur.atlassian.net/browse/PLAT-54718
> **Related:** [[PLAT-54719]] · [[PLAT-54720]] · [[PLAT-54730]] · [[PLAT-55258]] · [[PLAT-56165]] · [[PLAT-34075]] · [[PLAT-53429]] · [[PLAT-45655]]

## PRD Summary
Today Mission progress only updates after an order reaches **actual completion** (hotel checkout, flight landing), which hides progress from users who have already paid — driving CS inquiries and killing motivation for users with several in-flight orders. This epic introduces **Mission Pending Progress**: a **pending stamp** is generated and shown on the Mission Details page immediately when an order is **Issued** (payment complete), and it automatically **converts to an active stamp** once the stay/flight/activity is completed. Pending stamps count toward **visual progress only** — they never make a reward claimable until converted to Active.

## Key Learnings
- **Two-state stamp lifecycle:** Pending (order issued/paid) → Active (order actually completed). The pending state is a **hardcoded** static visual (processing indicator + "Pending" label), NOT a dashboard-configurable image asset.
- **Reward gating is the critical trap:** visual progress can look 100% full with pending stamps, but claiming must stay blocked until enough stamps are Active.
- **Time-window revalidation:** pending stamps only exist for orders expected to complete *within* the mission period. Editing the mission end date (extend/shorten) must revalidate and add/remove pending stamps.
- **Order lifecycle side effects:** refund removes the pending stamp; reschedule that pushes completion outside the period removes it, but a reschedule that stays inside the period retains it.
- **Display prioritization:** when active + pending exceed the required visible slots, active stamps fill the visible slots first and excess pending stamps are hidden (per the 5-stamp / 7-order worked example in the stories).
- **Enrolled vs unenrolled:** unenrolled eligible users still see pending stamps (same pending state); on conversion the active stamp uses the **active unenrolled** image variant.
- Reuses the Tiket Mission stamp/progress model from [[PLAT-34075]] (MVP) — "Pending Progress" was explicitly *out of scope* in the MVP PRD and is now delivered here.
